Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

The short version: your stuff stays on your device. Oh, There It Is! helps you remember where you put your belongings. Everything you save lives only on your own phone or computer.

What the app stores

  • Item names, locations, exact spots, categories, and notes you type or speak
  • Photos you take or choose for your items

All of this is saved in your device's own browser storage. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. We cannot see it — nobody can but you.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required, and none exists
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind
  • No data is sent to any server
  • Your photos are never used to train AI models

Voice input

If you use the microphone button, your device's built-in speech-to-text service converts your speech to text. That service is provided by your device or browser and is subject to its own privacy policy. The app only receives the finished text.

Backups

The backup button creates a file on your device containing your saved items and photos. That file is yours — the app never sends it anywhere. Anyone you give the file to could read it, so store it as carefully as you would the things in it.

Purchases

The optional Plus upgrade is sold through Google Play (in the Android app) or Stripe (on the web). Those companies process your payment under their own privacy policies — the app never sees or stores your payment details. If you unlock with a license key, the key is checked with our license server and kept only on your device.

Deleting your data

Delete any item inside the app, or use Settings → Erase everything to remove all data. Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data also removes everything.

Changes

If a future version adds optional features that work differently (such as accounts or syncing), this policy will be updated first and the app will explain the change clearly before anything is stored anywhere other than your device.
